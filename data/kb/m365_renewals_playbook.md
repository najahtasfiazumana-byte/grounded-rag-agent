# Microsoft 365 Renewals Playbook

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. This is an internal process playbook invented for this demo. It describes Kiara Tech's own process, not Microsoft's licensing rules.

## Renewal timeline

The renewal radar flags every Microsoft 365 subscription 90 days before its end date. The account owner must contact the client within 5 business days of the flag. A renewal quote should be sent no later than 30 days before the end date.

## Seat true-up

Before sending a renewal quote, compare the licensed seat count with the client's current headcount in the CRM. If headcount is more than 10% above licensed seats, propose adding seats in the renewal quote. If headcount is more than 15% below licensed seats, flag the account to the account manager because the client may reduce seats.

## Upgrade opportunities

Clients on Business Basic with more than 20 seats should be offered a Business Standard upgrade during renewal. Clients that handle sensitive data, such as clinics and law firms, should be offered Business Premium for its additional security features. Upgrade proposals are logged as a separate opportunity in the CRM so they can be tracked.

## Lapsed subscriptions

If a subscription lapses, the client keeps read-only access for a short grace period. Reps must treat a lapsed subscription as Priority 1 and call the client the same day. Lapsed renewals are reported in the weekly sales meeting.

## Renewal commission

Reps earn 3% commission on renewal value and 6% on new seats or upgrades added at renewal. Commission is paid after the client's payment is received.
