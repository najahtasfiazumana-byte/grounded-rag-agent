# Warranty and Returns Policy

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. All policies below are invented for this demo.

## Standard warranty

All laptops and desktops sold by Kiara Tech carry a 12-month carry-in warranty from the date of delivery. Monitors, docking stations and keyboards carry a 6-month carry-in warranty. Consumables such as toner, cables and adapters are not covered by warranty.

## Extended warranty

Clients can buy an extended warranty that brings total cover to 36 months for laptops and desktops. The extended warranty costs 8% of the device's selling price and must be purchased within 30 days of delivery. Extended warranty includes next-business-day onsite service in the Klang Valley.

## Returns and restocking fee

Unopened items can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened items that are not faulty can be returned within 14 days of delivery but carry a 15% restocking fee. Software licences, including Microsoft 365 and antivirus subscriptions, cannot be returned once the licence key has been issued.

## Dead on arrival

A device that is faulty when it arrives is treated as dead on arrival (DOA). DOA claims must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Kiara Tech replaces DOA devices with a new unit at no charge, and no restocking fee applies.

## How to raise a warranty claim

Sales reps raise warranty claims in the CRM by creating a Case linked to the client's account. The case must include the serial number, invoice number and a description of the fault. The service desk replies to every warranty case within 1 business day.
