# Support and Escalation Guide

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. All processes below are invented for this demo.

## Support hotline

Clients reach the service desk through the support hotline, the client portal or by email. The hotline is staffed during business hours only. After-hours support is available only to clients on a Premium Support contract.

## Premium Support contract

Premium Support costs MYR 2,400 per year for up to 25 devices, and MYR 80 per extra device per year. It adds after-hours phone support from 6:00 pm to 11:00 pm on weekdays and a dedicated support engineer. Premium Support clients get Priority 1 response within 1 hour.

## Escalation path

If a ticket misses its response time, the service desk lead is alerted automatically. If a Priority 1 ticket is still open after 8 hours, it is escalated to the head of operations. Sales reps should not promise fixes or timelines to clients; they should link the client to the open ticket instead.

## Remote support tools

The service desk uses an approved remote support tool, and engineers always ask for the client's permission before connecting. Clients should never be asked to share passwords by phone or email. Engineers never request payment details during a support session.
