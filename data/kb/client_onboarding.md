# New Client Onboarding

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. All processes below are invented for this demo.

## Account creation

Every new client needs an Account record in the CRM before a quote is sent. The account must include the company's SSM number, billing address, delivery address and at least one primary contact with an email address. Duplicate accounts must be merged before creating a new deal.

## Know your client check

For first orders above MYR 20,000, finance runs a know-your-client check before the order is accepted. The check confirms that the SSM registration is active and that the company is not on the internal blocked-clients list. The check usually takes 1 business day.

## Welcome pack

After the first paid order, the client receives a welcome email with their account manager's details, the support hotline and a link to the client portal. The account manager books a 30-minute onboarding call within 10 business days of the first delivery.

## Lead handover from marketing

Marketing-qualified leads are assigned to sales reps by territory. The rep must make first contact within 1 business day of assignment. Leads with no contact after 3 business days are automatically reassigned to another rep in the same territory.
