# Pricing and Discount Approval

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. All policies below are invented for this demo.

## Standard margin

The target gross margin is 12% on hardware and 25% on services. Quotes below 8% gross margin on hardware cannot be sent without a sales director's approval.

## Discount approval matrix

Sales reps can give up to 5% discount off list price without approval. Discounts above 5% and up to 10% need sales manager approval. Discounts above 10% need sales director approval. Approval is requested in the CRM on the deal record, and the quote PDF cannot be generated until the approval is recorded.

## Volume pricing

Orders of 50 devices or more of the same model qualify for project pricing from the distributor. The rep must request project pricing at least 3 business days before the quote is due. Project pricing is valid for 14 days and only for the named end client.

## Quote validity

Standard quotes are valid for 14 days. Quotes for items with volatile pricing, such as memory upgrades and storage, are valid for 7 days. After expiry the rep must re-check supplier prices before re-issuing the quote.

## Price matching

Kiara Tech can match a competitor's written quote for the same model and configuration if the competitor is an authorised reseller in Malaysia. Price matching counts as a discount and follows the same approval matrix.
