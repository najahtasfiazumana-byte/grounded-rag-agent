# Delivery and Service Levels

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. All policies below are invented for this demo.

## Delivery times

Stock items are delivered within 2 business days in the Klang Valley. Deliveries to other states in Peninsular Malaysia take 3 to 5 business days. Deliveries to Sabah and Sarawak take 7 to 10 business days. Items that are not in stock follow the supplier's lead time, which the rep must confirm before quoting a delivery date.

## Delivery charges

Delivery is free for orders of MYR 3,000 and above. Orders below MYR 3,000 pay a flat delivery fee of MYR 50 within Peninsular Malaysia and MYR 120 to Sabah and Sarawak.

## Rush orders

Rush same-day delivery is available in the Klang Valley for stock items when the order is confirmed before 11:00 am. The rush fee is MYR 150 per order. Rush orders require sales manager approval in the CRM before the warehouse will release the stock.

## Installation and setup

Basic setup, which covers unboxing, power-on test and Windows first-boot configuration, is free for orders of 10 devices or more. For smaller orders, setup costs MYR 45 per device. Imaging with a client's own Windows image costs MYR 80 per device and needs 5 business days' notice.

## Service level for support tickets

Priority 1 tickets (whole office down) get a response within 2 hours during business hours. Priority 2 tickets (one team affected) get a response within 4 business hours. Priority 3 tickets (single user, workaround available) get a response within 1 business day. Business hours are 9:00 am to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday, excluding Selangor public holidays.
