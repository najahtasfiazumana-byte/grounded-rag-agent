# Payment Terms and Credit

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. All policies below are invented for this demo.

## Standard terms

New clients pay 100% upfront until a credit account is approved. Approved credit accounts are on Net 30 terms, counted from the invoice date. Government agencies and GLCs are on Net 60 terms once their purchase order is received.

## Credit account approval

A client can apply for a credit account after 3 months of trading or after 3 paid orders, whichever comes first. The application needs the client's SSM registration, the last 6 months of bank statements and a signed credit application form. Finance decides within 5 business days. The default credit limit for a new account is MYR 50,000.

## Late payment

Overdue invoices carry a late payment charge of 1.5% per month on the overdue amount. Accounts that are more than 60 days overdue are put on credit hold, and no new orders are released until the balance is cleared. Sales reps are notified automatically when one of their accounts goes on credit hold.

## Accepted payment methods

Kiara Tech accepts bank transfer (DuitNow or IBG), cheque and corporate credit card. Credit card payments above MYR 10,000 carry a 1.2% card processing fee. Cash is not accepted.

## Tax

All prices are quoted before Sales and Service Tax (SST). Hardware is charged SST at 10% and services such as setup and imaging are charged SST at 8%. Quotes must always show the SST amount on a separate line.
