# Product Catalog Notes for Sales

> Fictional company: Kiara Tech Solutions Sdn Bhd. Product names and prices below are invented for this demo.

## Recommended business laptops

The Aero 14 Pro is the default recommendation for office staff. It has 16 GB RAM, a 512 GB SSD and a list price of MYR 4,299. The Aero 16 Studio is recommended for designers and engineers. It has 32 GB RAM, a 1 TB SSD, a dedicated graphics card and a list price of MYR 7,899.

## Budget option

The Nimbus 15 is the budget laptop for front-desk and call-centre staff. It has 8 GB RAM, a 256 GB SSD and a list price of MYR 2,349. Do not recommend the Nimbus 15 for staff who run more than one business application at the same time.

## Desktops

The Tower M5 mini desktop is recommended for shared workstations and reception counters. It has 16 GB RAM, a 512 GB SSD and a list price of MYR 3,150. It does not include a monitor.

## Monitors and accessories

The ViewLine 24 monitor has a list price of MYR 699 and the ViewLine 27 QHD has a list price of MYR 1,190. The UniDock USB-C docking station has a list price of MYR 549 and works with the Aero and Nimbus laptops. Always offer a docking station with every Aero laptop sold for hot-desking offices.

## Memory upgrades

The Aero 14 Pro can be upgraded from 16 GB to 32 GB RAM for MYR 420 per device. The Nimbus 15 has soldered memory and cannot be upgraded after purchase.
